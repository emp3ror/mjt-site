My Strava trace clocks about **21 km** and **~1,770 m** of combined climbing. I am keeping this GPX as the canonical field log for my day, while the official station plan above reflects the organiser's race sheet.

## Notes from the course

- Started in the late-morning block; air was already warm on the first climb out of Dollu.
- Heart rate sat mostly aerobic until the steeper pushes where cadence dropped and power-hiking took over.
- Saved the file for the elevation silhouette: the race is less about flat speed and more about pacing the rollers between official aid points without emptying the tank before the finish.
